# World geometry migration notes

Revision `worlds-v1` keeps the room, portal, spawn, interaction, layer, and original collider IDs from `data/room-specs.json`. Geometry was tuned in place to preserve those references. New interaction and collider IDs below are additive. No IDs were renamed.

## Geometry changes from the handoff room specs

- **Discovery Plaza:** increased the fountain collider radius to 155 units (183 units including participant radius); moved the welcome board collider to `(520,110,240,76)`, networking kiosk to `(190,230,230,170)`, event info to `(1500,230,230,170)`, and the four bench colliders to `(480,320,180,54)`, `(1260,320,180,54)`, `(500,720,180,54)`, `(1240,720,180,54)`. Moved the existing welcome and north bench interaction anchors to `(640,250)`, `(570,415)`, `(1350,415)`; added south-bench interactions at `(590,815)` and `(1330,815)`. Widened east/west portal triggers from 110 to 220 world units. Tuned return spawns to `from_garden (1600,560)`, `from_arcade (320,560)`, and `from_cafe (960,262)` so arrivals remain outside their portal thresholds.
- **Conversation Café:** moved baseline `default` and `from_plaza` `(960,890)` to `(960,790)` so entrants land on the clear floor below the counter. Added `cafe_table_west_second`, `cafe_table_center_second`, and `cafe_table_east_second`. Each table has two configured seat positions.
- **Connection Garden:** moved baseline `default` and `from_plaza` `(960,900)` to `(960,740)`, and moved the return trigger `garden_to_plaza` to `(820,820,280,180)` so the entire threshold is reachable without placing the Plaza arrival spawn in it. Reshaped `west-bridge` from its skewed 280×140 quadrilateral to a 370×280 rectangle and `south-bridge` to 550×250; both decks have at least 180 units of clear width. Added colliders `stream-west-bank`, `stream-east-bank`, `stream-south-west-bank`, `stream-south-east-bank`, `west-bridge-rail-west`, `west-bridge-rail-east`, `south-bridge-rail-west`, `south-bridge-rail-east`, and `south-bench`. The north rail of the south bridge starts at x=1020 to leave a clear junction where the two decks meet. Moved `garden_photo_spot` from `(1450,570)` to `(1640,520)` and added `garden_bench_east_second` at `(1510,800)` after its initial generated candidate `(1640,750)` fell outside reachable floor. Added `garden_bench_west_second`, `garden_bench_east_second`, `garden_bench_south`, and `garden_bench_south_second` interactions for the three bench pairs.
- **Activity Arcade:** moved baseline `default` and `from_plaza` `(1520,850)` to `(470,540)` to clear the game-floor colliders.
- **Idea Lab:** moved baseline `default` and `from_plaza` `(1530,850)` to `(1530,760)`. Added a room description for the existing project-board P0 interaction. Moved `lab_table_main` from `(960,700)` to `(800,700)`, added `lab_table_main_second` at `(1120,700)`, and configured two seats at each collaboration table.
- **All rooms:** added `seats: [{id,x,y,facing}]` with stable, individual seat IDs (`plaza_nw_1`/`2`, `plaza_ne_1`/`2`, `plaza_sw_1`/`2`, `plaza_se_1`/`2`; `cafe_west_1`/`2`, `cafe_center_1`/`2`, `cafe_east_1`/`2`; `garden_west_1`/`2`, `garden_east_1`/`2`, `garden_south_1`/`2`; `lab_main_1`/`2`, `lab_round_1`/`2`). Seat availability is now reserved per seat. These additions and geometry tuning are intentionally non-semantic and do not change room destinations or gameplay behavior.

The Garden rail positions were adjusted during implementation after the startup reachability validator found that the initial arrangement isolated the entry spawn. The final rails align to the long edges of both decks, with open ends and reachable entry paths. The generated ground art was adjusted to show the stream and decks; it remains a character-free placeholder layer and is not used as a collision map.
